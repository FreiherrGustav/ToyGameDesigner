Paint mode
Game rules
These aren't placed — they just switch on or off for the whole game the moment you click them. Gravity makes everything fall. Wrap makes an edge loop around to the opposite side instead of stopping you — one switch per direction.
Background
Pure decoration, zero gameplay meaning. Pick one to paint, or hit + to draw a new one — it's added to this project's kit, never swapped in for an old one.
Geometry
Modifier
Two independent choices, painted together. Geometry decides what blocks movement. Modifier decides what happens on contact, layered on top of whichever geometry you picked — none + none just clears a tile back to plain background.
Temperament (for actors the player runs into)
The player (only one)
This is the one actor you move. It has no temperament of its own — temperament only describes how the other actors react when this one touches them.
Grab a sprite
Starter art for whatever's selected above — grab one, then keep editing it below.
Pixel art (8×8) — editing: player
Geometry/modifier tiles use small built-in icons — no drawing needed, so they always read the same way. Backgrounds and actors are yours to draw. The grid is always live — no separate play mode.
