Toy Game-Maker

Game rules
These aren't placed — they just switch on or off the moment you click them. Gravity makes everything fall. Wrap makes an edge loop around to the opposite side instead of stopping you — one switch per direction. These rules, and the camera below, belong to whichever room is open, and switch automatically with the room tabs.
Camera
Fixed shows the whole room at once. Follow lets the room be wider than the screen and keeps the camera on the player. Runner does the same, but the camera moves on its own — fall behind it and that's game over.

Custom characters

How it acts —
Voice (optional)
Pick an object
Things that don't move on their own and do one specific thing the moment you touch them. Pushable block is yours to redraw, like a character; the rest use a built-in icon, same as Ground.
Collectibles
Things a kid scores by touching, then they're gone — grab one to place it, or hit + to draw your own.
Color
For a switch or plate — none: a switch flips gravity, a plate does nothing. Pick a color instead and it turns that color on (a switch keeps it on; a plate only while something's standing on it) — any matching-colored wall over in Ground listens for it.
For a door, color means something different: it's how the door decides where it leads. None keeps a door's old default (links onward automatically). A color no other door is using yet opens into a brand-new room. The same color on a second door links the two directly — no new room — which is how a real loop back to an earlier room gets built. Only two doors can ever share one color.
Ground
Surface
Two independent choices, painted together. Ground decides what blocks movement. Surface decides what happens on contact, layered on top — none + none just clears a tile back to empty.
Hazard costs one life and knocks you back — you keep going. Lethal costs every life at once, instantly, with no knockback — there's nothing left to fight through.
Ground textures
A real look for solid ground instead of a plain color — grab one to paint it, or hit + to draw your own.
Lethals
Lava and fire (both animated) and spikes — tagged lethal automatically the moment you place one: touch it and it's game over instantly, no knockback. Grab one to paint it, or hit + to draw your own (yours will be lethal too).
Double-click any painted Ground texture or Lethal to spin it 90° — just how it looks, not how it blocks or hurts.
Color
None: this wall always blocks, same as ever. Pick a color and it only blocks while nothing of that color is currently on — a matching switch or plate over in Objects is what turns it off.
Paint
Pure decoration, zero gameplay meaning. Pick one to paint, or hit + to draw a new one — it's added to this project's kit, never swapped in for an old one.
